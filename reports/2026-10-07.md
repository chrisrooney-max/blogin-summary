- **AI in Practice: a year of impact across the South African business unit** — A year-in-review of the South African BU's AI work, covering 150+ consultants trained, an LLM-assisted Java-to-Rust port at JLP, and AI governance and QA-agent work at AXA that gave a ~25% speed-up ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/ai-in-practice-a-year-of-impact-across-the-south-african-business-unit-326468))
- **AI in practice, in India** [POV candidate] — Vishwas Bhagath rounds up client outcomes from the India BU's AI work, including 145 IG apps uplifted with a 54% lower change failure rate, a ~25x contract-data-extraction uplift at BCG, and PPRO connector migrations cut from 60 days to 6-8 ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/ai-in-practice-in-india-326633))
- **What is Jev and why I believe it will be a groundbreaking shift in how we build using AI** — Ganesh Prasannah explains Jev, a very cheap "system one" model that returns structured JSON decisions with confidence scores in milliseconds, and suggests uses such as CI/CD failure triage, alert triage, injection detection and fraud analysis ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/what-is-jev-and-why-i-believe-it-will-be-a-groundbreaking-shift-in-how-we-build-using-ai-326488))
- **From meetings to pretty useful pictures** — Nathan Carney describes prompting Claude to turn a client workshop's unreadable whiteboard photos and transcript into an SVG-then-PNG diagram, saving an estimated 4-5 hours ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/from-meetings-to-pretty-useful-pictures-326466))
- **Determinism in a non-deterministic world** [POV candidate] — Nathan Carney tested splitting a 2M-token legacy case-management context into deterministic lookups (including DuckDB) for a DEFRA migration PoC, found it cut prompt size and time but total tokens only ~4%, and found it exposed contradictions and gaps in the existing analysis ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/determinism-in-a-non-deterministic-world-326455))
