- **Homeopathic Research and Synthetic Users** [POV candidate] — Matt Ballantine argues that LLM-generated "synthetic users" may suit mass-market products but not internal business change, because talking to real colleagues is what builds their stake in the outcome (illustrated by a CFO's sceptical assistant who became a platform advocate after helping build the case), and that LLMs should support interviews and analysis rather than replace them ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/homeopathic-research-and-synthetic-users-328101))
- **Engineers have to be marketers now.** — Simonne Visser argues that because LLMs make technical output near-free, enterprise software engineers have no moat and must make themselves legible (writing down their thinking, having arguable opinions) so customers buy the trusted human behind the work ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/engineers-have-to-be-marketers-now-328099))
- **Kubernetes on MacOS: Building a Multi-Node Cluster with Apple Container** — Shane Bedggood walks through using kiac and Apple's `container` runtime to run a local multi-node Kubernetes cluster where each node is its own VM, so you can rehearse node failure, Cilium/eBPF networking and PodDisruptionBudgets on a Mac ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/kubernetes-on-macos-building-a-multi-node-cluster-with-apple-container-328042))
- **Doxosophia: The Conceit of Wisdom** — Cronje van Heerden uses Plato's warning about writing to describe AI-generated work that is syntactically flawless but shallow ("doxosophia"), while suggesting engineers who spar with models may be developing cognitive skills we can't yet name or measure ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/doxosophia-the-conceit-of-wisdom-327914))
- **Are we going to be forced to use AI?** [POV candidate] — Cagri Yucel observes very uneven AI adoption across (mainly public sector) engagements, from engineers living in coding harnesses to teams banning AI outright, and asks how EE helps associates adapt before competitors with fully AI-capable teams win bids and tenders start asking for the percentage of AI-capable engineers ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/are-we-going-to-be-forced-to-use-ai-327903))
- **If people wouldn't recommend you, why would ChatGPT?** [POV candidate] — Russell Beech argues that a retailer missing from AI shopping recommendations has a reputation gap rather than a content gap (citing Ahrefs and Muck Rack data that AI mentions brands people talk about most), and proposes using Ehrenberg-Bass "category entry points" and experience-driven reviews to earn recommendations ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/if-people-wouldn-t-recommend-you-why-would-chatgpt-327900))
- **The Age of Custom Tools** — Richard Shephard argues AI has dropped the cost of building bespoke tools so far that the "rule of three" no longer applies, and offers a heuristic: have the LLM write a deterministic tool when the same input should give the same output, use the LLM directly when the task needs judgement ([link](https://equalexperts.blogin.co/posts/the-age-of-custom-tools-327781))
